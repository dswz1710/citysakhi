Your first journey

Start wherever you arrive.

Choose a city, select an airport, railway station or bus stand—or enter any starting location—and add your destination. Compare route options, review clearly labelled indicative fare ranges and find essentials near your destination. Estimates are guides, not live quotes; check current fares, schedules and official operator updates before travelling.

Not sure which station or bus stand? Check the station printed on your ticket before travelling. Some cities have multiple terminals.

CitySakhi opens map searches and provides indicative fare estimates when you enter a distance. It does not receive live cab quotes, live fares, schedules or traffic data.
