Your first journey

Start wherever you arrive.

Choose a city, select an airport, railway station or bus stand—or enter any starting location—and add your destination. Compare transport options, see indicative fare ranges and find food near where you are going.

Not sure which station or bus stand? Check the station printed on your ticket before travelling. Some cities have multiple terminals.

CitySakhi opens map searches and provides indicative fare estimates when you enter a distance. It does not receive live cab quotes, live fares, schedules or traffic data.
